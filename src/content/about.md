Hey, I'm **Z** — a developer who spends most of the day somewhere between a terminal
and an editor. I am locked in a constant, **bare-knuckle fight** with a compiler bent on
doing me harm, while its manager, the Time, ensures the bout remains in its favor.

This site is my **public _workspace_** and meeting place. Like those japanese bars where the
chef cooks at bare sight.

The idea is post any project im put myself into, talk about advances on them, talk about the new
challenges i assume and talking about topics i find interesting.

In addition to that, in the meeting side of this site, i will talk about the conferences i assist,
the events i go to, the colleagues i find and the eminences i talk to. Like those afternoons talks with
friends that begins with a simple text of _Wanna hang out?_ and ends talking the most intriquised gosspis
about that school partner that end up pregnant.

## What you'll find here

- **Posts** — Advances of projects, rabbits holes i had fallen on, achievments i have gathered, anything that
  could be intersting to chat about.
- **Projects** — things that got far enough to have a name. Here will be, not only the breaking news about that project,
  but also the link to access that project, a resume of the reason behind the project and the people implied, if there are
  more than only me .
- **Events** — talks, meetups and conference notes, with links to the slides when they exist. Also will be pictures of
  the event so you can see where i was 👀. Also, any meeting, quote or hard take i hear or assist, will be here.
- **Contact Me** — If you want to talk with me about a project im working on, give me you experience in a rabbit hole that
  i have recently fallen on, or simply say hi to me, here will be my RRSS and my email so you can contact me ^^.

## Why a personal site?

Because the internet forgets, and I'd rather keep my own notes than trust that
someone else's archive will still be up in five years. You will not getting any
annoying spam in your email, any intrusive notification in your cellphone, and
the navegation will always be ad free.
